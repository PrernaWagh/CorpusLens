Corpus analysis is an important part of natural language processing.
A corpus contains collections of text that can be analyzed using computational methods.
CorpusLens measures words, sentences, paragraphs, characters, and word frequencies.
Parallel processing can reduce execution time when large text collections are analyzed.
OpenMP allows programs to divide computational work across multiple threads.
Multiple threads can process different parts of a corpus at the same time.
Frequency analysis identifies the words that appear most often in the corpus.
Statistics help researchers understand the structure and characteristics of text.
Performance measurements allow us to compare sequential and parallel execution.
CorpusLens demonstrates how parallel computing can be applied to text analysis.
